# Change Management

## Content change
Examples: new item, enemy, recipe, or location. Requires relevant specialist review.

## System change
Examples: new skill, combat mechanic, progression resource, or economy rule. Requires design review, technical review, and decision log.

## Kernel change
Examples: changing solo-first, no-FOMO, mobile-only, or walking-first identity. Requires explicit owner approval.

Every major proposal should include:

- Change
- Reason
- Benefits
- Risks
- Alternatives
- Recommendation
