# ART-14 — Ruthless Creative Editor, FMPO02 Wave 1

Evidence: the four `pair_*.png` device renders, `pair_gfcp_*`, `enemies_stage_after_x2.png`, `atlas_life_review.png`. I did not build this; I owe it nothing.

## THE TEN — what makes this read as AI/template on a phone, in order

**1. One template, six times.** Every tab is label + place + teal boot chip, a 16:9 picture strip, a
column of dark cards to the bottom, six icons. Six pages, one page.
FIX: a different *structure* per tab — Combat full-bleed, World edge-to-edge, Craft a two-column bench.
DISGUISE (forbidden): a border family per tab. Eleven frames on one skeleton is still one page.

**2. The chassis is wallpaper.** The same leather frame, brackets and rivet pitch wraps a five-line
list, a button pair and a portrait alike; applied everywhere it means nothing.
FIX: frame *one* element per screen — the thing the screen is about — bare ground for the rest.
DISGUISE: shrink it and lower its contrast. Then it is quiet wallpaper.

**3. Void.** The bottom third of Adventure and Combat is flat empty rectangle.
FIX: run the scene image down behind the content so the screen bottom is world, not nothing.
DISGUISE: pad it with a divider or a tip card. Filler admits the defect.

**4. Craft and Skills are a database printout.** Equal-height rows, 48px icon left, two text lines,
affordance right, repeated to the fold. That is a settings list.
FIX: the ready item becomes a *thing on a bench* — large, off-grid, ingredients beside it; the rest
collapse to a dense one-line index. Two registers, not thirty rows.
DISGUISE: alternating tints, per-row ornament, a busier row. Still thirty rows.

**5. Every button is the same dark rounded rectangle.** Craft, Track as Pursuit, Gather ×1, Goal
Board, Field Notes, Step Tracker, Equip, Unequip — one control, centred label.
FIX: one committed action per screen carries weight and material; the rest become text or a mark.
DISGUISE: bevels and gradients. A prettier uniform is still a uniform.

**6. Item icons are one recipe.** Object centred, same 3/4 angle, same rim light, same soft shadow,
same fill ratio: Oak Handle and Oak Plank are two brown bars, every food is a bowl.
FIX: vary what the icon is a *picture of* — planks stacked past the edge, broth in a steaming pot, rations bound in cloth. Framing and scale per class, not colour per class.
DISGUISE: rarity glow, corner brackets, palette shift. The silhouettes still match.

**7. Creatures stand in a vacuum.** Nine animals in strict side profile, near-identical height, one
lighting, floating on flat ground; in the encounter the wolf sits in a huge blank box.
FIX: crop the preview to the creature's real footprint and put it in its place — forest floor, at the
scale it will fight at, cut by the frame edge.
DISGUISE: vignette or texture the box. Still a specimen photo.

**8. The skill hues are an app palette, not a world palette.** Full-chroma pink/green/blue/orange
ribbons behind Cooking, Foraging, Mining, Smithing — the generated-dashboard tell.
FIX: derive each ink from its material (herb, copper, hearth-coal, oak) at world chroma, carried as a
thin edge or the icon plate — never a full-width ribbon.
DISGUISE: desaturate the same four hues. The same arbitrary set, quieter.

**9. The atlas has no focus and its weather is pasted on.** Uniform stamp density corner to corner, one
repeated tree stamp, hex tessellation in the ice, a straight forest wall west, layer-cake bands south, hard-edged cloud stickers on the terrain.
FIX: authored variation of density and value — massed dark forest interiors, thinned ragged margins,
haze dropping contrast with distance — so the eye is led rather than flooded.
DISGUISE: scatter more props on top. Density is the disease, not the cure.

**10. The Traveler never changes.** Bronze plate in Inventory; white shirt and green vest in Adventure,
gathering and combat. Gear that never appears is a spreadsheet entry.
FIX: project `Equipment.bySlot` into every scene showing the man — one armour-class state reused across
gather, travel and combat.
DISGUISE: an equipment badge or a "wearing:" caption. Text about a picture.

## KILL LIST — not one generation on these

- Eleven border families; screens differ by band, surface and picture (L-18), not by frame.
- Corner ornaments, filigree or dividers on every card — ornament everywhere is texture.
- Sparkle, glow-pulse, particle burst or count-up on rewards; slot-machine feel is an anti-feature.
- A busier chassis or another chassis re-roll — batches C–I already burned thirteen generations.
- Redrawing accepted enemies. Bear, boar, salamander, ram and the Frost Lynx are fine.
- 200 ambient sprites at the wrong density. Ten at the correct plane beat two hundred.
- A new tab, nav icon set or tab renaming; recolours sold as variety; text baked into a raster panel.
- Any atlas repaint not single-region and device-verified; portrait re-rolls; new persisted state.

## WOW LIST — five things worth two minutes each

1. **Combat goes full-bleed.** Forest fills the phone, the command frame becomes flat text actions on a
   translucent foot, wolf and Traveler large and offset in depth. No brown box, no void.
2. **The Traveler wears his gear.** He walks into the mine in the bronze plate just crafted, and the
   same figure stands in the meadow and in the fight.
3. **Craft becomes a bench.** Herb Broth as a real pot on wood with its ingredients beside it, one
   committed action, the other 35 recipes collapsed to a scannable index.
4. **The map gains weather and distance.** Frostmere fades into haze, the forest darkens inward and
   frays at its margin, the west wall and southern banding gone.
5. **The world is inhabited.** A caravan on the road, birds over the marsh, smoke leaning off Haven's
   Rest, the red wyrm reading as a wyrm above the volcano — at the map's own density.

## THE IDENTITY TEST

Project Stride looks like a walked place drawn small: dark warm-grey ground, deep green and
copper-bronze earth, one reserved teal that means steps and nothing else. Its art is made objects and
real places — a pot on a bench, an animal on ground it lives on, a map with weather — never an icon on a plate or a specimen in a box. If a piece could drop into another mobile RPG unnoticed it does not belong here; if it survives only by being framed, ornamented or glowing, nor does it.
