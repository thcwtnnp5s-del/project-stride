# EPO03 — producer's read of the atlas, with eight regions accepted

Judged on the packaged composite at `assets/art/v1/world/atlas_base.png`
(regions S1, S2, S3, SA1, L3, L2, L1, WA), full canvas and at crops. This is a
desk read staging the owner's device pass, not a substitute for it.

## What has clearly changed since 59c4723

- **The south is a coast, not a band.** The pale stripe that ran the full width
  at y 810–870 is gone. The shore bends, a spit hooks south-east, braided
  channels run into tidal flats, dunes sit at an angle, and the delta reads as
  a delta reaching the sea. This was the round's P0 and it is closed.
- **The forest's west face is broken open.** The vertical canopy wall at
  x≈256 is now bays, a promontory and stepping copses (WA) — the defect
  FMPO02 could not reach because it lay inside the protected core.
- **Three destinations exist where three stickers were.** The fairy glade
  reads as a castle grown from birches over a light-ringed pool; the storm
  house as a black gable among blasted trees above the beach; the ice bastion
  as a mound with a causeway. All three are painted into the terrain, so
  unlike props they survive the overview zoom.

## What still reads wrong, ranked — the brief for the territories not yet run

1. **The ice front, roughly 620–860 × 40–290 (PROD-WORLD-EAST).** The
   strongest remaining seam on the map. The shelf meets open water along a
   hard, near-straight white diagonal, and the pack ice north-east of it is a
   regular honeycomb that repeats at a glance — a generator's tell. This wants
   a calving front: bergs of several sizes, brash ice thinning seaward, leads
   opening at irregular angles, a graded shelf face with shadow. It is the one
   remaining boundary that would make a viewer say "pasted".
2. **The ice-to-volcano join at y≈280 (PROD-WORLD-EAST).** Ice and black rock
   still butt rather than meet. Wants ash-dusted snow, cooled rock and steam
   where the two meet — and the volcano's own cliff and towers are identity,
   held by goldens, so the transition is authored *around* them.
3. **The far west, 0–130 × 300–700 (PROD-WORLD-WEST, in progress).** Still
   open green with scattered pebble dots and a road that loops without a
   reason. WB is addressing it.
4. **The north snowline (PROD-WORLD-NORTH).** Where drifts meet the western
   foothills and forest there are still cut-like runs; wants altitude —
   pines thinning upward, rock showing through, a cold palette progression.
5. **The east sea.** A quarter of the canvas is nearly empty water. The answer
   is not acreage or terrain but authored life on it — a ship on a lane, a
   whale surfacing, gulls, a reef shoal. Assigned to PROD-WORLD-LIFE.

## Standing instruction

The core master — Haven's Rest, the wheat, the delta, Frostmere, the
Worldspine, the volcano — remains the best painting on the map and the
identity anchor. Nothing in the list above is a licence to touch it beyond
the transitions that meet it.
