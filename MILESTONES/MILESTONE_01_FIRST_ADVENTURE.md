# Milestone 01 — First Adventure Vertical Slice

## Goal

Prove that real-world walking can power a satisfying solo RPG loop.

## Required loop

```text
Walk
Apply movement
Travel or gather
Gain resources and XP
Craft or equip
Fight
Improve
Repeat
```

## Starter world

- Haven’s Rest
- Whispering Woods
- Stonefall Mine
- Forgotten Hollow

## Initial skills

- Woodcutting
- Mining
- Foraging
- Smithing
- Cooking

## Initial combat

- Forest Wolf
- Cave Goblin
- Hollow Guardian

## Required screens

Six navigation tabs:

- Adventure
- Character
- Skills
- Inventory
- Craft
- World

Plus one full-screen modal:

- Combat — presented from Adventure or World, dismissible only by resolving or retreating

## Required platform systems

- HealthKit step permission
- Step reconciliation
- Local save
- Offline state
- Audio and haptic hooks

## Explicit exclusions

- Multiplayer
- Trading
- Guilds
- PvP
- Monetization
- Large world
- Complex live economy

## Success test

A player walks, returns, understands what changed, uses the results to improve their character, completes an active encounter, and wants to continue.
