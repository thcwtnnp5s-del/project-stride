# Economy and Resource Model

```text
Movement
Gathering
Raw resources
Processing
Crafting
Equipment and supplies
Adventure
New opportunities
```

Initial resource layers:

- Raw: logs, ore, herbs, food ingredients
- Processed: handles, ingots, prepared components
- Finished: tools, weapons, armor, consumables

Currency may support convenience and NPC transactions in later milestones, but it should not replace skill and resource progression.

**Milestone 01 has no currency and no merchant economy.** Every resource comes from movement; every item comes from crafting. NPCs exist for onboarding, quests, lore, and atmosphere, but do not buy or sell. A shop at this stage would short-circuit the exact loop the vertical slice exists to validate. See `DECISIONS/0004_MILESTONE_01_SCOPE.md`.

Resource scarcity should create decisions without forcing repetitive busywork.
