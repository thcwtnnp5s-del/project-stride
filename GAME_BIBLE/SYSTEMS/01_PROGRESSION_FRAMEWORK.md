# Progression Framework

Progression has three layers:

## Character progression

Persistent character level, attributes, equipment capability, and account-wide unlocks.

## Skill progression

Long-term mastery in gathering, production, exploration, and combat-related capabilities.

## World progression

Regions, destinations, quests, bosses, reputation, and discoveries.

Early progression should teach quickly. Mid progression should create meaningful decisions. Late progression should feel prestigious without invalidating old accomplishments.
