# Crafting System Framework

```text
Gather
Process
Create
Use
Improve
```

Crafting transforms movement-earned resources into meaningful capability.

## Crafting costs no steps

Crafting resolves instantly once the player has the materials. It is not a step sink.

The steps were already spent gathering the materials. Charging for them again would make crafting feel like a toll booth between the player and the reward they already walked for, and it would turn the Craft screen into a place where progress goes to wait. The player walks to *get* things; crafting is where they *use* what they got.

This also keeps the "steps gate rate, never access" rule honest: any recipe the player can afford is always craftable, at any step balance.

Initial outputs:

- Tools
- Weapons
- Armor
- Food
- Utility items

Every recipe must answer:

- Why does the player want this?
- What system does it support?
- What decision does it create?
- What future recipe path does it lead toward?
