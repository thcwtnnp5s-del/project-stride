# Core Game Loop

```text
Plan
Walk
Discover
Progress
Adventure
Plan again
```

## Plan

The player selects a meaningful goal such as travel, mining, woodcutting, crafting preparation, or boss preparation.

## Walk

Real-world steps advance the selected plan.

## Discover

The player returns to clear outcomes: travel completed, resources earned, an encounter unlocked, or an expedition advanced.

## Progress

The player uses gains to level skills, craft, equip, unlock, and plan.

## Adventure

The player enters active solo PvE, makes strategic choices, explores, or completes a meaningful encounter.

## Session lengths

- Micro: 30 seconds to 2 minutes
- Short: 5 to 10 minutes
- Long: 20+ minutes

Walking creates opportunity. Decisions create progression. Active play creates memories.
