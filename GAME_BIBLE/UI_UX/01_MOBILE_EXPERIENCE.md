# Mobile Experience

The app should feel like checking a living adventure journal.

The player should understand within seconds:

1. Where am I?
2. What am I doing?
3. What changed?
4. What should I do next?

## Initial navigation

- Adventure
- Character
- Skills
- Inventory
- Craft
- World

## Principles

- One-handed use
- Clear hierarchy
- Large touch targets
- Short-session support
- Minimal menu depth
- Strong progress feedback
- Calm, atmospheric presentation
- No spreadsheet-like overload
