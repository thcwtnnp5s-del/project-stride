# Audio Identity

Audio is a core gameplay system.

## Pillars

- Adventure
- Satisfaction
- Atmosphere
- Material identity
- Readable feedback

Each region needs:

- Ambient bed
- Wildlife and environmental detail
- Weather variation
- Distinct musical identity

Each major action needs:

- Initiation sound
- Material response
- Reward confirmation
- Optional haptic pairing

Copper should not sound like iron. Oak should not sound like pine. A mine should not sound like a forest.
