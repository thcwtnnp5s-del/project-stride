# Design Pillars

## Walking creates opportunity

Movement should unlock choices and progress, not merely convert directly into generic experience.

## Respect the player’s time

The game waits for the player. Absence never creates punishment.

## Meaningful progression

The player should always understand what they are working toward and what a choice unlocks.

## Interconnected systems

Skills, crafting, resources, travel, combat, and equipment should reinforce each other.

## Sensory satisfaction

Audio, animation, visual feedback, and haptics are gameplay.

## Mobile first

Design for short sessions, touch input, one-handed use, clear hierarchy, and health integration.

## Expandable foundation

New skills, regions, items, enemies, and expansions should extend systems rather than replace them.

## Depth over complexity

Prefer simple rules with rich interactions.

## Player agency

Walking should create decisions rather than automate the entire game.

## Wonder

The world should occasionally surprise the player with memorable discoveries and atmospheric details.
