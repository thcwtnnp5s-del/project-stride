# Player Promise

Project Stride promises:

> Your real-world effort will create meaningful progress, and the game will respect the life you live outside it.

The player can:

- Take breaks without losing progress
- Ignore notifications without penalty
- Play for seconds or for longer sessions
- Choose gathering, crafting, combat, or exploration goals
- Return to a world that feels welcoming rather than demanding

The game should create curiosity, not obligation.
