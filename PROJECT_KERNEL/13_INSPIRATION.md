# Inspiration Guide

## WalkScape

Borrow:
- Movement-driven progression
- Travel
- Mobile-first immersion
- Passive progress

Avoid:
- A purely passive experience
- Copying UI or content directly

## Melvor Idle

Borrow:
- Long-term skill growth
- Interconnected resources
- Idle-style *planning* — set a goal, close the app, collect later
- Character progression

Avoid:
- Spreadsheet-only presentation
- Systems detached from world fantasy
- **Wall-clock accrual.** Melvor's activities tick on time; Stride's tick on steps. Borrow the planning feel, not the clock. See `DECISIONS/0001_PROGRESSION_CLOCK.md`.

## Old School RuneScape

Borrow:
- Skill interdependence
- Item purpose
- Familiar places
- Long-term achievement

Avoid:
- Grind without meaningful unlocks

## New World

Borrow:
- Gathering sound identity
- Environmental ambience
- Material-specific feedback

Avoid:
- MMO infrastructure and live-service complexity
