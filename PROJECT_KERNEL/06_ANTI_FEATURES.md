# Anti-Features

Do not introduce without explicit owner approval:

- Premium currencies
- Advertisements
- Loot boxes or gacha
- Battle passes
- Daily-login streaks
- Expiring rewards that punish absence
- Energy systems designed to restrict play
- Punishing idle decay
- Mandatory multiplayer
- Trading economy
- Guild requirements
- PvP
- Live-service pressure
- Grind whose only purpose is larger numbers
- Generic mobile-game engagement mechanics
